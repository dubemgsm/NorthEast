Data Sources

  • School Locations & Coordinates: GRID3 (Geo-Referenced Infrastructure and Demographic Data for Development), circa 2018-2020.
  • School Operational Status: iMMAP / Nigeria Education Cluster, "North East Nigeria School List", Status as of June 2019. (Available via Humanitarian Data Exchange).
  • Population Data: National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) & National Population Commission (NPC), 2022 LGA Population Projections.
  • Conflict Data: ACLED (Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project) / UCDP (Uppsala Conflict Data Program). Filtered for events occurring between January 1, 2020, and December 31, 2024.
  • IDP Locations: IOM DTM (Displacement Tracking Matrix) Nigeria, Site Assessment Round 50, April 2026.
